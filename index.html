Our Story

A new name on a familiar corner.

Orchard Trail Nutrition is the new chapter of a club that has been part of the Romeo and Washington Township routine for years.

In August 2026 the shop changed hands — new owners Jeff and Laura took the keys, and the club was renamed Orchard Trail Nutrition. Same faces behind the counter, same shakes and teas, a fresh name over the door.

It is a simple idea, done properly: real fruit flavors, blends made when you order, and a room where the regulars know each other. This fall the club also joined the Northern Macomb Regional Chamber of Commerce — a small business, firmly rooted in the neighbourhood it serves.
